- **Location:** [verify_state_machine_parity.py](../../../scripts/verify_state_machine_parity.py#L51-L63) and [verify_state_machine_parity.py](../../../scripts/verify_state_machine_parity.py#L86-L92); cf. `_exit_status` in [cli.py](../../../src/messpy/cli.py#L823-L833) and [parity-negative.log](./parity-negative.log#L1-L12).

### Optional Design Preferences (Non-Bugs)

1. **`@dataclass(frozen=True)` on in-place mutating collector classes in `analyzer.py`:**
   - `_FindingEmissions` ([analyzer.py](../../../src/messpy/analyzer.py#L802)), `_GlobalBindings` ([analyzer.py](../../../src/messpy/analyzer.py#L1066)), `_FirstAccesses` ([analyzer.py](../../../src/messpy/analyzer.py#L1413)), `_DictionaryKeys` ([analyzer.py](../../../src/messpy/analyzer.py#L2367)), `_SymbolTablePool` ([analyzer.py](../../../src/messpy/analyzer.py#L2666)), `_ClosureUses` ([analyzer.py](../../../src/messpy/analyzer.py#L3067)), `_ClassTraversal` ([analyzer.py](../../../src/messpy/analyzer.py#L3445)), and `_SourceDiscovery` ([analyzer.py](../../../src/messpy/analyzer.py#L5118)) are decorated with `@dataclass(frozen=True)` even though their `collect` classmethods mutate their `set`, `list`, or `dict` attributes in place.
   - Using plain `@dataclass` (as already done for `_CallReachability` and `_FirstCallEdges` in [callgraph.py](../../../src/messpy/callgraph.py#L34-L144), `_ConstantKinds` in [constants.py](../../../src/messpy/constants.py#L23), `_EffectReports` in [effects.py](../../../src/messpy/effects.py#L128), `_ActionPropagation` in [onion.py](../../../src/messpy/onion.py#L243), and `_AttributeEvidence` / `_VisitorInheritance` in [analyzer.py](../../../src/messpy/analyzer.py#L3243)) would more accurately distinguish mutable collectors from immutable value states.
2. **Scope of `_SourceDiscovery` in `M06`:**
   - `_SourceDiscovery.collect` ([analyzer.py](../../../src/messpy/analyzer.py#L5118-L5154)) owns file and error deduplication across top-level CLI roots in `_source_files`, while recursive directory descent in `_collect_directory_source_files` ([analyzer.py](../../../src/messpy/analyzer.py#L5165-L5185)) still accumulates raw `set[Path]` and `list[ProcessingError]` locals and passes `root: Path` as a parameter.
3. **Caller-enforced terminal guards on `_ComprehensionFrame.advance`:**
   - `_EvaluationPrefix._include` ([reachability.py](../../../src/messpy/reachability.py#L38-L39)) and `_ComprehensionBindingUse.following_clause` ([analyzer.py](../../../src/messpy/analyzer.py#L2907-L2908)) self-guard their terminal state (`STOPPED` / `shadowed`), whereas `_ComprehensionFrame.advance` ([ownership.py](../../../src/messpy/ownership.py#L1230-L1239)) relies on the caller loop ([ownership.py](../../../src/messpy/ownership.py#L1257-L1260)) to break when `not frame.active`.

### Focused Audit of Requested Risk Areas

1. **Analyzer collection owners & pre-dedup materialization (`M38`):**
   - Verified in `_FindingEmissions` ([analyzer.py](../../../src/messpy/analyzer.py#L802-L823)) and `_FirstAccesses` ([analyzer.py](../../../src/messpy/analyzer.py#L1413-L1422)). First-witness semantics (`setdefault` on access name, first `id(scope)` for `ExitExpression`, first binding line per unused variable name) are preserved and covered by `test_emission_deduplication_keeps_first_binding_and_first_exit_per_scope` and `test_repeated_bindings_and_exits_construct_only_emitted_findings` ([test_state_analyzer.py](../../../tests/test_state_analyzer.py#L206-L227)).
2. **Source discovery accumulation (`M06`):**
   - Verified in `_SourceDiscovery.collect` and `_source_files` ([analyzer.py](../../../src/messpy/analyzer.py#L5118-L5154)). Streaming the generator `( _source_files_under(...) for path in paths )` into `_SourceDiscovery.collect` avoids holding all per-root file sets simultaneously, preserves first-error-per-path deduplication (`error.path in discovery.error_paths`), and sorts discovered files deterministically by `candidate.as_posix()`.
3. **Linked traversal performance (`M09`, `M22`, `M24`, `M32`, `M33`, `M37`):**
   - Verified that commits `3a03151` and `8efd1da` eliminated tuple-slicing and tuple-stack copying in favor of $O(1)$ indexed cursors and singly linked frames:
     - `_CallableLookup` ([callgraph.py](../../../src/messpy/callgraph.py#L279-L298)) and `_NameLookup` ([names.py](../../../src/messpy/names.py#L126-L141)) advance an integer `position` over a shared `scopes` tuple in $O(1)$ per step.
     - `_CaptureFrame` / `_CaptureWalk` ([captures.py](../../../src/messpy/captures.py#L69-L96)) chains frames via `previous: _CaptureFrame | None` and decrements `position` from `len(nodes) - 1` down to `0`, matching the exact preorder `pending.pop()` sequence of the baseline in $O(N)$ total time and $O(D)$ stack depth.
     - `_TraceFrame` / `_ObservationReplay` ([ownership.py](../../../src/messpy/ownership.py#L640-L664)) chains nested trace tuples via `previous: _TraceFrame | None` and increments `position` from `0` to `len(trace) - 1`, replaying `_Seen` observations in exact chronological order in $O(N)$ time without allocating `reversed(item)` copies.
     - `_AliasSnapshot` / `_CallableAliases` ([effects.py](../../../src/messpy/effects.py#L86-L126)) and `_EvaluatedNode` / `_EvaluationPrefix` ([reachability.py](../../../src/messpy/reachability.py#L15-L50)) prepend linked nodes in $O(1)$ and reverse once at materialization in $O(N)$.
4. **Lexical resolver differences (`M09`):**
   - Verified that the four resolvers remain intentionally distinct where their domain semantics differ:
     - `_NameLookup` ([names.py](../../../src/messpy/names.py#L126-L177)) includes `ClassDef` only for expressions directly in the class body (`_with_parent_scope`) and hides module imports when the module assigns to the same name.
     - `_CallableLookup` ([callgraph.py](../../../src/messpy/callgraph.py#L279-L314)) always skips `index.class_ids` and invalidates module-level callable bindings when any function in the module mutates the name via `global` (`index.rebound_globals`).
     - `_ShadowLookup` ([analyzer.py](../../../src/messpy/analyzer.py#L1328-L1353)) starts with `allow_class=True` for direct class-body expressions and transitions `allow_class=False` upon crossing any function, class, or comprehension boundary, and stops with `verdict=False` if an enclosing function declares `global name`.
     - `_ScopeChain.binding` ([analyzer.py](../../../src/messpy/analyzer.py#L1898-L1910)) prepends `class_scope` only via `for_node` ([analyzer.py](../../../src/messpy/analyzer.py#L1882-L1896)) when the node is directly inside a class body.
5. **Cache publication (`M36`, `M39`):**
   - `_ScopeUsageMemo.resolve` ([analyzer.py](../../../src/messpy/analyzer.py#L3091-L3100)) returns `(self.entries[id(table)], self)` without copying on a cache hit, and on a miss folds child memos iteratively through `_ClosureUses.collect` before publishing `id(table): usage`.
   - `_State.begin` and `_State.remember` ([ownership.py](../../../src/messpy/ownership.py#L251-L258)) invalidate stale per-node `evaluated` entries before re-evaluating a node (critical in loop rounds) and record new evaluations.
   - `_CallMemo.capture` and `_CallMemo.replay` ([ownership.py](../../../src/messpy/ownership.py#L260-L275)) capture cell and heap deltas by identity (`before.cells.get(key) is not value`) after `_execute_body` completes and replay them onto caller state while keeping `calls` excluded from `_State` equality (`compare=False` at [ownership.py](../../../src/messpy/ownership.py#L240)).
6. **Suppression ordering (`M25`, `M26`):**
   - Verified in `_SuppressionSchedule` and `_SuppressionScan` ([analyzer.py](../../../src/messpy/analyzer.py#L4949-L5011)). `state.before(finding.line, schedule)` consumes only directives with `directive_line < finding.line` (so same-line `# messpy-disable` does not suppress a finding on its own line, while `disable` followed by same-line `enable` or nested `disable` depth $\ge 2$ behaves identically to baseline). `_next_line_suppression` ([analyzer.py](../../../src/messpy/analyzer.py#L5038-L5054)) skips comment lines with unrelated comment findings and expands across multi-line definition headers (`header_lines`).

### Limitations

- Per instructions, no heavy test suites, fuzzing runs, or CrossHair symbolic executions were re-executed during this review; verification relies on static inspection of the git diffs (`3448957..933a1bc` and `933a1bc..a5b7ca4`), uncommitted working-tree changes, unit tests, and the captured audit logs ([integrated-tests.log](./integrated-tests.log), [integrated-parity.log](./integrated-parity.log), [parity-negative.log](./parity-negative.log), [ruleset-formal-before.log](./ruleset-formal-before.log), [ruleset-formal-after.log](./ruleset-formal-after.log), and [ruleset-formal-negative.log](./ruleset-formal-negative.log)).
- Differential parity ([verify_state_machine_parity.py](../../../scripts/verify_state_machine_parity.py)) and bounded CrossHair contracts cover a finite corpus of 518 source samples and 4 symbolic `_merge` properties, not unbounded equivalence over all Python programs.
